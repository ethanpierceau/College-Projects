# Brayton–Rankine Combined-Cycle Power Plant

**Focus:** Thermodynamics · Thermal Design · Power Systems · Energy Analysis

## Objective
Propose a natural-gas combined-cycle plant with a **100 MW electrical output objective** for a data center by recovering Brayton turbine exhaust heat in a Rankine steam cycle.

## Engineering work
- Calculated thermodynamic properties, work and heat transfers across Brayton and Rankine cycle states.
- Considered compressor pressure ratio, turbine inlet temperature, mass flow and isentropic efficiencies.
- Evaluated a heat-recovery steam generator and candidate turbine, condenser and heat-exchanger equipment.
- Estimated fuel consumption, heat rejection, carbon dioxide emissions and economic/environmental tradeoffs.

## Reported design figures
| Variable | Reported value |
|---|---:|
| Plant target | 100 MW |

| Brayton pressure ratio | 15 |
| Air mass flow | 91.00 kg/s |
