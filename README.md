# Mechanical Engineering | College Projects

A portfolio of **five collaborative engineering projects** exploring mechanical design, robotics, mechatronics, controls, thermodynamics, prototyping, and technical analysis.

**Core tools and methods:** SolidWorks · MATLAB · Arduino/C++ · 3D printing · finite element analysis (FEA) · motion simulation · state-space modeling · engineering drawings.

## Projects at a glance

### Automated IV Bag Lift and Monitoring
A load-cell-driven motorized lift intended to reduce repeated overhead adjustments of IV bags. The prototype combines an Arduino Nano, HX711 load-cell amplifier, guided carriage and mechanical drive. Testing demonstrated automatic motion and revealed a motor-to-gear coupling failure. **[Read details →](projects/automated-iv-bag-lift/)**

### Drill-Powered Stair Climber
A redesign study of a drill-powered stair-climbing mechanism, combining SolidWorks CAD and a detailed drawing package with manufacturing information, bill of materials and a motion study. **[Read details →](projects/drill-powered-stair-climber/)**

### Klann Six-Bar Walking Robot
Kinematic and dynamic study of the Klann walking mechanism, extended to an eight-legged robot assembly. Examined a walking foot path, torque demand under contact loads and robot speed versus motor RPM. **[Read details →](projects/klann-six-bar-walking-robot/)**

### Rotary Inverted Pendulum
Derived coupled dynamics using Lagrangian mechanics and analyzed a rotary inverted pendulum in MATLAB. Designed full-state feedback and explored cascaded PD control. Standalone simulation met approximate control targets, but results did **not** agree with the supplied reference model. **[Read details →](projects/rotary-inverted-pendulum-control/)**

### Brayton–Rankine Combined-Cycle Power Plant
Conceptual natural-gas combined-cycle design recovering turbine exhaust heat into a Rankine steam cycle. Evaluated operating states, component selection, mass flow, environmental tradeoffs and the reported cycle efficiency. **[Read details →](projects/brayton-rankine-power-plant/)**

## Technical skills

- **Design and analysis:** SolidWorks, CAD assemblies, tolerancing, fabrication drawings, free-body diagrams, FEA, rigid-body dynamics, motion studies.
- **Programming and controls:** MATLAB, Arduino/C++, sensor calibration, transfer functions, state-space modeling, pole placement, Bode plots, root locus.
- **Thermal and power systems:** Brayton/Rankine cycles, heat recovery, thermodynamic property analysis, energy balances.
- **Engineering practice:** Concept selection, budgets/BOMs, prototyping, testing, design tradeoffs, failure analysis and collaboration.

## About these projects

These are **collaborative academic engineering projects**, not independent commercial or certified designs. Descriptions reflect the shared reports and presentations and do not attribute every action to one person. Design targets are distinguished from measured and simulated results.
